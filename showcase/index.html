START WITH THE CUSTOMER DECISION

Why this
project matters.

Security analysts, procurement teams and risk owners need to understand which supplier evidence supports the next due-diligence step, and which gaps need follow-up.

THE WORKFLOW PROBLEM

Fragmented evidence.

Questionnaires, PDFs and scans arrive with inconsistent structure. Reviewers must locate answers, interpret ambiguity, preserve sources and explain the assessment.

THE PRODUCT RESPONSE

A usable review record.

A local pipeline turns supported documents into checked answers, structured suggestions and human decisions. The application connects the evidence to the action a reviewer needs to take.

THE VALUE TO VALIDATE

Less effort. Better decisions.

Test total review time, corrections, unsupported positive assessments and follow-up effort with users. The prototype has technical evidence; customer outcomes remain to be measured.

04 / CUSTOMER STRATEGY & ADOPTION

A pilot plan,
with a decision at each stage.

A proposed plan for a hypothetical customer: define the operational question, work through the evidence with analysts, train reviewers, measure value, and decide the next scope together.

  1. Discover the workflow

    Meet security, procurement, risk and engineering stakeholders. Observe the handoffs, identify relevant inputs, and establish baseline measures.

  2. Configure and demonstrate

    Agree the rubric and criticality boundaries. Show extraction, checked mappings, a model correction and the risk summary using fictional examples.

  3. Train and evaluate

    Guide reviewers through a complete questionnaire. Measure review time, correction effort, evidence errors and independent use.

  4. Iterate on the highest-value gap

    Use actual feedback to choose the next improvement and name its owner. Agree acceptance criteria before expanding deployment scope.

Practical tooling, with clear responsibilities.

LM Studio serves the local Open Weight AI model. Obsidian provides explicitly selected AI rules, knowledge and persistent project memory. Python and JavaScript implement the pipeline and review application; Swift and Node support PDF extraction/OCR; GitHub Pages delivers the public demo. Docker and oMLX are optional, untested extensions.

고객의 공급업체 온보딩 문제를 데이터 파이프라인과 검토 애플리케이션으로 연결합니다. PDF의 텍스트와 스캔 내용을 로컬에서 추출하고, 검토자가 답변과 출처 페이지를 확인한 뒤 AI 평가를 실행합니다. AI의 제안과 사람의 최종 판단을 따로 저장하여 수정 이력을 남깁니다.

이 공개 데모는 가상 공급업체의 저장된 결과를 사용합니다. 실제 AI 실행은 LM Studio에서 Open Weight AI model을 사용하는 로컬 앱에서 이루어집니다. 25개 합성 평가 중 24개가 초안 정답과 일치했으나, 실제 정확도나 규정 준수를 보장하는 결과는 아닙니다.
