# Assessment rules — public sample

These are reviewer instructions, not supplier evidence. Use the application's four-status rubric and exact source citations.

- An unanswered question means Unknown; it does not establish that a control is absent.
- Distinguish a policy requirement from evidence of current implementation. If operations explicitly describe an existing practice with insufficient frequency or coverage, assess the stated operational gap as Partly met. A policy target alone does not prove the practice occurs.
- Treat contradictory factual statements as unresolved. Do not invent missing supplier facts.
- Preserve source quotes and let the analyst accept, correct or request evidence.
